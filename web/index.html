Everything you need

Native capture, zero clutter

The essentials of a screenshot tool in one tidy drop-down. Nothing more.

Fullscreen, window & area

Capture the whole screen, a single window, or drag over any region. Same results as native macOS, but one click to reach.

File or clipboard

Save a PNG through a save dialog that opens once the screen is clear, or copy straight to the clipboard to paste into chats and tickets.

Timed captures

A 5-second delay gives you time to open menus, tooltips and hover states before the shutter fires. Fullscreen, window or area.

Silent mode

Turn off the shutter sound for screenshots only, without muting your call, your music or the rest of the Mac.

Launch at login

Tick Autolaunch and the camera icon is there after every restart. No Dock icon, no windows.

Open in Preview or Finder

Open each capture in Preview to mark it up, or reveal it in Finder. Open Last Screenshot brings the latest file back at any time.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is ScreenshotMenu?

A free, open-source macOS menu bar app for taking screenshots. It puts nine capture modes (fullscreen, window or area, to a file, to the clipboard or on a 5-second timer) in one menu, so you don't need to remember keyboard shortcuts.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT licence, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases. It runs on macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

How is it different from the built-in screenshot tool?

It uses the same engine, Apple's screencapture, so images are identical. The difference is access: every mode is one click away in the menu bar, file captures always ask where to save, and Silent Mode mutes only screenshots.

What permissions does it need?

Only Screen Recording, which macOS requires for every app that captures the screen, Apple's own included. The prompt mentions system audio because one permission covers both; the app never records video or audio. It doesn't ask for Accessibility and has no network code.

Does it have annotation, OCR or scrolling capture?

No, deliberately. Tick Open in Preview to mark up captures with arrows, shapes and text. For blur, OCR, scrolling capture or a capture history, a full suite such as Shottr or CleanShot X fits better.

Is this the ScreenshotMenu on the Mac App Store?

No. There's an older, unrelated ScreenshotMenu by another developer on the Mac App Store, last updated in 2017. This one is distributed as a signed, notarised DMG from GitHub.

How do I uninstall it?

Untick Autolaunch, choose Quit from the menu, and drag ScreenshotMenu from Applications to the Trash. You can also remove it from System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording.
